About Nova AI

Nova AI is a simulated AI made with JavaScript.

It can explain built-in topics, do basic calculations, remember your name, and help with certain questions.

Nova does not use a real AI model and cannot directly read the internet. If it doesn't know something, it will tell you instead of pretending.
